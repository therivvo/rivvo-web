# Bubble Bumper — AAA Art Direction Contract

## Status

v036 rejected by user as still low-poly/massinha. Do not promote any prior Sol pass as AAA approval.

## Reference bar

The source concept is the authority: a polished cartoon kart product render, not a primitive blockout. The target must preserve the same silhouette, proportions, color blocking, cockpit/personality, front U bumper and compact exposed rear assembly in all five views.

## P0 visual requirements

1. Continuous primary shell from nose → cockpit shoulders → side pods → rear termination.
2. Side pods are thick protective bodywork with a physically recessed blue inset inside a saturated yellow frame; no floating capsules or sticker-like panels.
3. Driver is seated inside a deep cockpit: pelvis, thighs, torso, neck, helmet shell, visor, arms, gloves, wheel hub and steering column are connected and readable.
4. Front bumper is one true U-shaped tube in plan, with constant section, real rearward returns, collars and chassis sockets; it cannot read as a bar or external hoop.
5. Front and rear wheel centers are anchored to the chassis; rear tires are visibly larger/wider; hubs, rims, uprights, arms, dampers and fender clearances are readable.
6. Rear is a compact exposed mechanical module immediately behind the driver: central housing, two deep exhaust outlets, lower grille, short crossbar with yellow end caps, mounts and braces.
7. Secondary detail must be physical: panel gaps, recess depth, seams, bevels, fasteners, spring coils, exhaust lips, hub rings and tread/surface breaks.
8. Materials are separated by function: saturated blue paint, yellow paint, matte black rubber/structure, dark visor/cockpit, neutral metal, darker engine metal. Color changes must coincide with geometry boundaries.
9. All proof views are orthographic with common scale, wheel-center alignment, constant margins, neutral blueprint floor and a single 3/4 perspective only for appeal.

## Anti-failure rules

- No sphere-head/mannequin driver.
- No ellipsoid pod as final bodywork.
- No flat plate pretending to be an inset.
- No bars/tubes terminating without sockets.
- No large generic white/gray rear blob.
- No uniform plastic material.
- No Sol submission before internal vision passes this contract.

## Required evidence

- colored five-view contact sheet against concept;
- clay/black-fill five-view sheet proving silhouette and gaps;
- close-up crops: cockpit, pod inset, front U/socket, rear engine;
- build marker and artifact read-back;
- internal vision PASS before Sol;
- Sol PASS only after internal PASS;
- user approval before export/runtime.
